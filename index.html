About Ind-Legal

Comprehensive Expertise.
Proven Results.

Ind-Legal is a premier full-service law firm founded in 2004 and headquartered in New Delhi, supported by a strong network of affiliates across India.

For more than 20 years, we have delivered swift, practical, and dependable legal solutions to corporates, government bodies, and individuals. Our work spans litigation, arbitration, corporate advisory, real estate, finance, intellectual property, environmental law, labour and employment, insolvency, and strategic transactional matters.

Every engagement is guided by a client-centric approach that combines bespoke legal strategy with deep domain insight — focused on outcomes that are clear, effective, and oriented to long-term value.
